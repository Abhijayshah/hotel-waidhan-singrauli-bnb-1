# PHASE 4 — Remaining Website Pages & Technical SEO

## Role

Act as a senior Next.js developer, technical SEO specialist, and content architect for a local hospitality business.

## Objective

Complete the next stage of Hotel Waidhan Singrauli BNB by implementing the remaining approved public pages, strengthening technical SEO, and connecting the website through a clear internal-linking structure.

## 1. Inspect the existing project

Before making changes:

- Review the completed Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3 reports.
- Read the reference documents in `docs/reference/`, especially the sitemap, SEO strategy, keyword mapping, content guide, and technical SEO checklist.
- Inspect the current App Router routes, metadata helpers, navigation, sitemap, robots configuration, and shared components.
- Preserve existing functionality and design decisions.
- Do not recreate pages that already exist or overwrite working components unnecessarily.

## 2. Implement these approved routes

Build the following pages using reusable layouts and components:

### Business and information pages

- `/pricing` — Room rates and pricing information
- `/booking` — Booking and enquiry entry point
- `/corporate-stay` — Corporate and long-stay enquiries
- `/location` — Location, directions, and nearby destinations
- `/dining` — Dining and meal-service information
- `/gallery` — Property photo gallery
- `/amenities` — Facilities and amenities
- `/reviews` — Guest feedback and review links
- `/faq` — Frequently asked questions
- `/about` — About the property
- `/contact` — Contact and enquiry information

### Travel guide pages

- `/guide/places-to-visit-in-singrauli`
- `/guide/where-to-stay-in-waidhan`
- `/guide/how-to-reach-waidhan`

### Guest policy and legal pages

- `/privacy-policy`
- `/terms`
- `/cancellation-policy`
- `/guest-policies`

Implement only the routes in the approved reference sitemap. If the sitemap's route inventory conflicts with an existing implementation, document the conflict and preserve the established URL plan unless a deliberate correction is justified.

Do not create empty pages just to satisfy the route list.

## 3. Follow the keyword-to-page mapping

Use `05-keyword-to-page-mapping.csv` and the approved SEO strategy as the source of truth.

Examples of the intended search themes:

- `/pricing`: hotel room prices in Waidhan
- `/booking`: book a hotel room in Waidhan
- `/corporate-stay`: business accommodation in Singrauli
- `/location`: hotel near Waidhan Bus Stand
- `/guide/places-to-visit-in-singrauli`: places to visit in Singrauli
- `/guide/where-to-stay-in-waidhan`: where to stay in Waidhan
- `/guide/how-to-reach-waidhan`: how to reach Waidhan

For every page:

- Assign one primary search intent.
- Use secondary keywords naturally.
- Create a unique title, meta description, and H1.
- Provide genuinely useful, page-specific information.
- Link to relevant rooms, booking, location, and contact pages.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and overlapping pages targeting exactly the same intent.

Do not invent competitor facts, travel distances, transit schedules, fares, reviews, or property features. Mark unverified information for confirmation.

## 4. Pricing and booking pages

### Pricing

- Present a readable comparison of approved room and bed rates.
- Identify whether each price is nightly, per bed, or monthly.
- Explain inclusions and taxes only when confirmed.
- If the owner has not verified a price, show a clear enquiry-for-current-rates message instead of publishing a guessed amount.
- Do not advertise discounts or promo codes as active unless their terms are confirmed.

### Booking

- Provide a clear enquiry form or connect to the existing Phase 3 enquiry component.
- Include room type, dates, guest count, and contact details as appropriate.
- Validate user input.
- Explain that submitting an enquiry does not automatically confirm a reservation.
- Do not claim live inventory or instant confirmation without a working backend.
- Do not process payments in this phase.

## 5. Location and corporate pages

### Location

- Use the configured business address and map URL.
- Provide a Google Maps directions link.
- Embed a map only if the integration is configured correctly.
- Publish distances and journey times only after verification.

### Corporate stays

- Explain the intended corporate enquiry process.
- Include a dedicated contact form or enquiry CTA.
- Mention GST invoices, internet speeds, power backup, work desks, or other facilities only when confirmed by the owner.
- Do not invent a GSTIN or guarantee proximity to industrial sites without verified data.

## 6. Dining, amenities, gallery, and reviews

- Show dining information only if the service and availability have been confirmed.
- Display actual property photographs where available.
- Use honest placeholders when photography is missing.
- Never represent stock imagery as photographs of the actual hotel.
- Display only authentic, authorized testimonials or review excerpts.
- Provide a direct link to the relevant Google Business Profile review flow where configured.
- Do not fabricate ratings, review counts, awards, or endorsements.

## 7. Travel guides

Write helpful, readable travel information for:

- Places to visit in Singrauli.
- Choosing an accommodation area in Waidhan.
- Reaching Waidhan by road, rail, and local transport.

Requirements:

- Use clear headings and concise sections.
- Explain relevant travel logistics without unsupported claims.
- Verify changing or factual details before publication when source material is insufficient.
- Link naturally to the hotel's rooms, location, and contact pages.
- Avoid thin, repetitive SEO articles written only to target keywords.

## 8. Guest policies and legal pages

Create readable pages for:

- Privacy policy.
- Terms and conditions.
- Cancellation and refund policy.
- Guest check-in policies.

Do not invent legal business details, refund promises, personal-data practices, or regulatory registrations. Identify information that requires owner or professional review. Clearly distinguish draft policies from confirmed operational policies.

## 9. Technical SEO

Review and implement the appropriate Next.js features:

- Unique page metadata.
- Canonical URLs.
- Open Graph metadata.
- `src/app/sitemap.ts`.
- `src/app/robots.ts`.
- Breadcrumb navigation.
- Semantic HTML.
- Correct heading hierarchy.
- Descriptive internal links.
- Useful image alt text.
- Valid structured data supported by visible page content.

Use appropriate schema types only where justified by the actual page and business facts. Do not generate fake review markup or unsupported FAQ structured data.

Ensure only real, published, canonical public routes are listed in the sitemap. Keep private, administrative, or transactional utility routes out of the public sitemap where appropriate.

Check for duplicate titles, duplicate descriptions, broken internal links, and competing keyword targets.

## 10. Shared design and accessibility

Reuse the existing design system, shared header, footer, buttons, forms, and responsive behavior.

Maintain:

- Mobile-first layouts.
- Keyboard accessibility.
- Visible focus states.
- Readable contrast.
- Responsive tables and cards.
- Correct form labels and validation.
- Working navigation across all implemented pages.

Do not introduce a separate design system or unnecessarily duplicate CSS.

## 11. Testing

Run the available:

- Lint checks.
- TypeScript checks.
- Production build.
- Route and internal-link checks.
- Metadata and canonical checks.
- Sitemap and robots checks.
- Responsive and basic accessibility checks.

Fix issues caused by this phase. Record any checks that could not be completed. Never report a test as passed unless it was actually executed.
