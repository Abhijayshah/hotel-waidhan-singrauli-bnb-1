## 4. Google Search Console Integration

Prepare a reliable workflow for reviewing actual Search Console data.

Analyze, when data access is available:

- Search queries.
- Impressions and clicks.
- Click-through rate.
- Average position.
- Indexed pages.
- Pages excluded from indexing.
- Crawl errors.
- Search appearance and enhancement issues.
- Performance by device.
- Performance by landing page.

Prioritize relevant local search intent, including the approved target keywords for hotel rooms, affordable accommodation, dormitory, PG accommodation, and stays in Waidhan and Singrauli.

Do not claim a keyword ranks in a particular position unless the data supports it.

If account access is unavailable, create a reusable report template and instructions for exporting the required data.

## 5. Google Analytics 4 Analysis

Review real analytics data where available.

Analyze:

- Landing-page sessions.
- Organic search traffic.
- Accommodation page engagement.
- Call clicks.
- WhatsApp clicks.
- Directions clicks.
- Booking enquiry starts.
- Successful booking enquiries.
- Form errors and abandonment, where reliably measurable.
- Device and channel differences.

Distinguish between engagement events and actual business outcomes.

Do not treat a call click or WhatsApp click as a confirmed booking. Do not infer revenue from enquiries.

Verify event definitions and remove duplicate or incorrectly triggered events.

Never transmit guest phone numbers, names, emails, private enquiry details, or internal notes to analytics.

## 6. Local SEO Improvement Workflow

Review the current local SEO implementation and approved keyword mapping.

Identify improvements supported by actual evidence, such as:

- Missing or weak title tags.
- Unhelpful meta descriptions.
- Incomplete internal linking.
- Duplicate page intent.
- Broken internal links.
- Unindexed public pages.
- Poor mobile usability.
- Missing descriptive image text.
- Structured data errors.
- Inconsistent contact information.
- Outdated public content.

Keep each page aligned with its intended search intent.

Do not create numerous near-duplicate location pages or generate repetitive keyword-stuffed content.

Do not promise a Google ranking or fabricate competitor results.

## 7. Content Optimization

Review existing accommodation and informational pages against the approved strategy.

Improve content where needed for clarity, usefulness, and customer confidence.

Prioritize:

- Clear accommodation descriptions.
- Accurate contact and directions information.
- Transparent enquiry and confirmation steps.
- Useful information for corporate and transit travelers.
- Genuine answers to common guest questions.
- Helpful travel information relevant to Waidhan and Singrauli.
- Clear calls to action.

Use only owner-confirmed prices, facilities, room counts, policies, distances, and services.

Flag unverified claims for owner confirmation rather than publishing them as facts.

Do not create fake reviews, testimonials, certifications, awards, or customer stories.

## 8. Conversion Rate Optimization

Review the actual visitor journey from landing page to enquiry submission.

Inspect:

- Call-to-action placement.
- Mobile call and WhatsApp actions.
- Accommodation navigation.
- Form field count and clarity.
- Validation errors.
- Form submission speed.
- Trust information.
- Directions and contact accessibility.
- Confirmation messaging.

Prioritize improvements based on observed evidence rather than personal preference alone.

For each proposed change, document:

- Problem observed.
- Supporting evidence.
- Proposed improvement.
- Expected user benefit.
- Measurement method.
- Risk and rollback plan.

Implement small, reversible improvements first.

Do not introduce fake urgency, misleading availability indicators, fabricated discounts, or deceptive booking patterns.

## 9. Structured Optimization Experiments

Create an experiment register.

Each experiment should include:

- Unique experiment ID.
- Hypothesis.
- Target page or journey.
- Baseline measurement, if available.
- Proposed change.
- Primary metric.
- Guardrail metric.
- Start date.
- Evaluation criteria.
- Outcome.
- Follow-up action.

Examples include improving a room-page call to action, clarifying the booking enquiry form, or improving internal links between accommodation and location pages.

Do not claim an experiment succeeded without sufficient evidence.

Avoid A/B tests that lack enough traffic to produce meaningful conclusions. When traffic is low, use usability testing, qualitative feedback, and sequential measurement instead.

## 10. Booking Operations Feedback

- Common enquiry statuses.
- Repeated submission failures.
- Notification failures.
- Frequently requested accommodation types.
- Missing information that prevents staff from responding.
- Potential workflow bottlenecks.

Do not infer room demand, occupancy, or revenue without reliable records.

Do not expose customer information in public reports or analytics.

If no real enquiry history exists yet, establish a baseline process instead of fabricating findings.
