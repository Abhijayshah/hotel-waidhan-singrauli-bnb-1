How to Reach Us:

From Waidhan Bus Stand:

Walk 300 meters towards Ambedkar Chowk. Hotel entrance is prominently situated on the main commercial avenue.

From Singrauli Railway Station (Morwa):

Direct auto-rickshaws and cabs run frequently (18 km, 25 mins). Ask the driver for Ambedkar Chowk Waidhan.

From NTPC Vindhyanagar:

Take the 4-lane Highway directly into Waidhan center (10 km, 15 mins).
