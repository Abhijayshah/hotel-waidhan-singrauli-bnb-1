About Hotel Waidhan BnB

The premier hospitality destination in Singrauli, combining spotless hygiene, round-the-clock comfort, and unmatched affordability near Waidhan Bus Stand & NTPC Vindhyanagar.

Our Heritage & Promise

Built to Serve Travelers with Genuine Warmth and Absolute Dignity

Singrauli is famously known as the Energy Capital of India — a thriving industrial hub powered by colossal enterprises like NTPC Vindhyanagar, Northern Coalfields Limited (NCL), Reliance Sasan Power, and Essar. Every day, thousands of engineers, consultants, plant managers, students, and traveling families arrive in Waidhan seeking a clean, safe, and welcoming refuge.

Hotel Waidhan BnB was founded on a simple yet unshakeable commitment: no guest should ever have to compromise between cleanliness and affordability. Whether you occupy a single ₹349 dormitory bed or our top-tier Executive AC Suite, you receive the same spotless white-linen guarantee, high-speed Wi-Fi, uninterrupted 24/7 power backup, and respectful staff assistance.
