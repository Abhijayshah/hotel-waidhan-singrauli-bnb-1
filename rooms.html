Help & Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Our standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out is happily accommodated subject to room availability; simply give us a quick call at +91 78790 28316 before arrival.

Is Hotel Waidhan BnB couple-friendly and safe for solo travelers?

Yes, 100%! We welcome all guests above 18 years of age with valid government photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, or Passport). We maintain strict privacy, 24/7 CCTV security, and zero disturbance.

Do you offer GST corporate invoices for plant and company reimbursements?

Yes, we provide official GST-compliant tax invoices for corporate clients visiting NTPC Vindhyanagar, Northern Coalfields Ltd (NCL), Reliance Sasan, Essar, and government departments.

What payment methods are accepted at the hotel?

We accept all standard payment modes including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cash, Debit/Credit Cards, and Corporate Net Banking.
